Web Apps with Michael

I run a side business building websites and web apps for people and small businesses. It brings together the two parts of my background that interest me most: understanding what someone is trying to accomplish, and building something useful around that need.

Visit my business website

What I build

I work on custom marketing sites, landing pages, redesigns, and web apps. A website might help someone explain their services and turn interest into an inquiry. An app might handle a more specific workflow: an estimator, dashboard, booking tool, or client portal.

I approach those as different kinds of problems. A business that needs a clear service page and contact form does not automatically need a complex application. I want the scope to follow the job the software needs to do.

How I take a project from conversation to launch

  1. Understand the business. I start with the person, their customers, and the action the website or app should make easier. That gives me a practical definition of what a useful result looks like.
  2. Define the work. I turn that conversation into a scope, a proposed approach, and an estimate. I want the client to understand what is being built before the project gets underway.
  3. Build with feedback. I use AI coding tools extensively to develop designs, implement features, and work through problems. I share progress and iterate on the actual experience rather than leaving every decision until the end.
  4. Check the complete experience. I work through the important user paths, forms, mobile layouts, and integrations. Where a rule can be tested, I use a repeatable check. A page looking finished is only part of being ready to launch.
  5. Launch and support. I handle deployment and the practical details of getting the work online, then support changes and fixes as the project is used.

My current projects use tools including Next.js, TypeScript, and Vercel. I also work with data storage and service integrations when the app needs them. I am continuing to develop my engineering skills while using AI as part of how I build.

I also built the system behind the business

Alongside the client-facing work, I have built a private AI-assisted sales workflow for my own business. It connects prospecting, lead qualification, personalized email outreach, demo sharing, and the handoff to video calls.

I also offer this system commercially, so I keep its source code, prompts, and internal configuration private. I can describe the workflow and the decisions behind it without distributing the implementation.

  1. Find potential customers

    I use AI-assisted research to identify businesses that may be a fit for website or app work.

  2. Qualify the opportunity

    My workflow assesses the business context, available contact information, and whether there is a relevant need worth a conversation.

  3. Prepare and send outreach

    My system connects personalized email outreach to the lead record, with an offer grounded in the information available about that business.

  4. Share a relevant demo

    I make the offer concrete with an example of what I can build, so the conversation is about something a prospect can actually explore.

  5. Coordinate a video call

    My workflow carries the prospect's context into the scheduling handoff and the conversation about their needs, scope, and next steps.

The aim is to connect the work around a sale, from finding an opportunity to having an informed conversation.

Why this matters to my Dreambase application

Running this business makes me think about more than the code on a page. I have to connect a customer's problem, a clear offer, a working product, and the process around delivering it.

Building my own AI-assisted workflow has also made the handoffs important to me: what information moves from research to a message, what makes a lead worth pursuing, and what context needs to survive into the next conversation. That is part of why Dreambase interests me. I want to keep developing software that turns information into a useful next action, with enough context to understand why that action makes sense.
