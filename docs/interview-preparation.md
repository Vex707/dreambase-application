# Be ready to explain the work

## The contribution question
Say which parts you specified, prompted, reviewed, tested, changed, and deployed. Distinguish your existing workplace projects from these AI-assisted demo editions. “I used AI tools to build this” is useful context; follow it with the decisions you understand and can defend.

## Five explanations to practice
1. Why splitting CSV on commas fails for a quoted address containing a comma.
2. Why two addresses with different apartment units must survive deduplication.
3. Why a missing sale price is different from a zero-dollar value.
4. Why a schema-valid AI response can still make a wrong visual judgment.
5. Why joining an order to two line items can double a revenue total, and how a test could catch it.

## Honest boundaries
The demos use synthetic records. The audit replays invented model output. No live provider request, geocoding verification, production agent orchestration, or cross-tenant authorization test is demonstrated. Those are separate engineering tasks, not hidden capabilities.

## Questions for Dreambase
- Which agent failure class is currently the most expensive for users?
- How do you version business metric definitions alongside their evaluation cases?
- Where does a human enter the workflow when sources disagree or a result is uncertain?
- What would useful contribution in the first month look like for someone with my background?

## A useful answer when you don't know
“I haven't implemented that in production. Here's how I understand the problem, here's the part I've tested, and here's what I'd need to verify before relying on it.”
